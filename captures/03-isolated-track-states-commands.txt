Isolated stopped-state captures for protocol correlation

Common settings: Saleae Logic, 8 MS/s, digital channels 0-7, 8-bit SPI analyzers, MSB first, CPOL=1, CPHA=0, active-low enable. SPI-1 was MOSI-only (CH1/3/4); SPI-2 used MOSI CH5, provisional MISO/SDO2 CH2, clock CH6, enable CH7. The physical channel-2-to-SDO2 connection still needs continuity verification.

The track positions above are inferred from the known starting position and acknowledged step counts; there is no independent player track-status command. The adapter's `sensor status` reported `PLAY_LED_OFF` before and after a later `play`/`stop` test, so IR reception by the player is not independently verified. These files are useful for comparing stream patterns, not as ground-truth display state labels.
